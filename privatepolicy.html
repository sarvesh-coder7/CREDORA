PRIVACY POLICY.

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how CREDORA collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use our website and loan application management services.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Important: This is a starter privacy policy. Before publishing, ensure it accurately reflects your actual data collection, storage, security practices and applicable legal requirements.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, CREDORA may collect:

  • Name, email address and phone number.
  • Loan application information and requested loan amount.
  • Employment, income and other details you submit.
  • Documents uploaded to support a loan application.
  • Messages, enquiries and feedback.
  • Technical information such as browser type and error logs, if enabled.

Please submit only information that is necessary for your application and documents you are authorised to share.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Operate and maintain the CREDORA website.
  • Display and manage loan applications.
  • Organise uploaded documents.
  • Generate reports and application summaries.
  • Respond to your questions and support requests.
  • Improve website security and prevent misuse.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with lenders when you choose to submit or authorise an application, with service providers that help operate the website, or when disclosure is required by law.

Lenders and third-party service providers may have their own privacy policies. Please review them before sharing your information with those organisations.

CREDORA should identify any actual third-party hosting, analytics or document-storage providers in this policy before the website is launched.

4. Data Storage and Security

We aim to use appropriate safeguards to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

If your current website stores data in browser local storage, that information may remain on the device and browser where it was entered. It may not automatically be shared with other users or devices. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to access it.

Browser local storage should not be treated as secure production storage for sensitive financial information or loan documents.

5. Data Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy and for any applicable legal requirements.

Actual retention periods depend on how the CREDORA service is configured.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, or withdraw consent where applicable.

You may choose not to provide optional information, although some website features may then be unavailable.

If information is stored locally in your browser, clearing browser data may permanently remove saved application information.

7. Age Requirements

Loan-related features are intended for adults who are legally eligible to use them. You must be at least 18 years old, or meet any higher applicable legal age requirement, to use these features.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, data practices or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
